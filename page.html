About Book Haven

Founded in 1995, Book Haven has been serving the community as both a library and bookshop for over 25 years. Our mission is to foster a love of reading and provide access to knowledge for all.

With over 50,000 titles in our collection and a dedicated staff of book lovers, we strive to create a welcoming space for readers of all ages.

Our Facilities

  • Reading rooms with comfortable seating
  • Children's storytime area
  • Study rooms available for reservation
  • Bookshop with new releases and classics
  • Café serving coffee and light snacks
